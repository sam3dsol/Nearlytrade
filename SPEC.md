# nearly.trade launchpad contracts: specification for audit

Source of truth: `contracts/factory/src/lib.rs`, `contracts/locker3/src/lib.rs`, `contracts/token-tax/src/lib.rs`, `contracts/token-raw/src/lib.rs`. Amounts are yoctoNEAR unless a unit is given; "bps" is basis points of 10,000; TGas numbers were measured on neard 2.13.4 (protocol 86) against the real `dclv2.ref-labs.near` v2.3.14 and `wrap.near` wasm, and the factory's `const _` assertions re-check the gas arithmetic at compile time.

## 1. System overview

**Accounts**

| Account | Code | Role |
|---|---|---|
| `nearlytrade.near` | factory | Creates tokens, pools and positions; books and pays every fee; holds all admin. `owner_id` is this account itself (`get_owner`). |
| `lock`, `lock2`, `lock3`, `lock4`, `lock_4`, `lock_5` (`.nearlytrade.near`) | locker (v1, v2, then four builds of v3) | Own every launch's DCL position. The factory picks the locker by launch id from a schedule (`get_lockers`: lock from id 0, lock2 from 158, lock3 from 1810, lock4 from 1931, lock_4 from 1935, lock_5 from the cutoff set at the final deploy). All have zero access keys. lock3, lock4 and lock_4 run earlier revisions of `contracts/locker3`; lock_5 runs this tree's `contracts/locker3` and is the one in scope. |
| `<slug>.nearlytrade.near` (`pepe`, `pepe-2`, …) | token-raw or token-tax, referenced as a global contract by code hash | One NEP-141/145/148 token per launch, fixed supply minted once to the locker, no keys, no owner methods. |
| `dclv2.ref-labs.near` | Rhea DCL (third party) | Concentrated-liquidity exchange holding every pool and position. The factory keeps a per-launch exchange schedule (`get_dcls`) for a future migration. |
| `wrap.near` | wNEAR (third party) | Every NEAR-pair launch is quoted in wNEAR; the factory wraps for dev buys and unwraps refunds. |
| tax seller (`get_tax_seller`, currently `feeswap.near`) | plain account, off-chain bot | Receives tax tokens from `process_tax`, sells them on its own, and reports the proceeds back. |
| relayers (`get_relayers`, at most 16) | plain accounts, off-chain | May trigger a parked dev buy and refund a sniped one on the creator's behalf. |
| fee routers (`get_fee_routers`, at most 32), launch hook (`get_launch_hook`) | third-party contracts | Optional: a launch whose payee is an approved router is paid by plain transfer (NEAR or token), then told `on_launch_fees`; the hook is notified of every completed launch. |
| protocol recipients (`get_fee_split`), house creators, per-launch recipients | plain accounts | Where the protocol share is pushed; owner-set. |

**Who may call what (factory)**

- Anyone: `launch` (payable), `resume` at every step except DevBuy (and except AddLiquidity while the launch's first buy is still to go out with its position, see section 2), `claim_fees`, `push_creator_fees`, `push_creator_token_fees`, `push_creator_quote_fees`, `push_router_fees`, `split_protocol_token_fees`, `split_protocol_quote_fees`, `burn_token_fees`, `push_owed`, `collect_tax`, `refund_failed` (pays the creator whoever calls), `settle_first_buy` (books from the locker's record only), every `get_*` view.
- The launch's creator: `cancel_stuck_launch`, `cancel_dev_buy`, `claim_creator_fees` (own balance), `claim_creator_token_fees` and `claim_creator_quote_fees` (creator or the launch's payee).
- Creator, owner or a listed relayer: `resume` at DevBuy; the same three signers gate the in-line dev buy.
- Owner or a relayer: `refund_dev_buy`.
- Keeper (`assert_keeper`: the owner or the factory account itself, which are the same account today): `buyback`, `pay_holders`, `process_tax`, `pay_tax_holders`.
- Tax seller: `tax_proceeds` (payable) and `ft_on_transfer` with a `{"tax_proceeds":id,"sold":"n"}` msg; any other `ft_on_transfer` is refunded in full, and so is a settlement in a pair token whose swap lock is held (the seller retries).
- Owner (`assert_owner`, predecessor == `owner_id`): every `set_*`, `withdraw_*`, `sweep_wnear`, `locker_withdraw_dcl_asset`, `push_protocol_fees`, `seed_slug_counts`, `backfill_fee_opts`, `set_house_buyback`, `set_range`, `set_step`, `reset_inflight`, `release_dev_buy`, `locker_refund_buy`, `upgrade`, `add_fc_key`, `delete_fc_key`, `propose_owner`/`transfer_ownership`. Because the owner is the factory account, an owner call is a call the factory signs to itself with a function-call access key (section 5).
- The factory account only (`#[private]`): every `on_*` callback and `migrate`.

**Locker (lock_5)**: `add`, `add_buy`, `refund_buy`, `claim`, `withdraw_dcl_asset`, `set_dcl`, `register` are factory-only; `on_*` are private; the rest are views. **Token**: `tax_take` is admin-only (the factory); `ft_resolve_transfer` is private; `burn` and `storage_unregister` act on the caller's own balance only; there is no mint, no owner, no pause.

## 2. Launch flow

**`launch(args)`**, attach `quote_launch(icon_bytes, dev_buy, tax).total` and 300 TGas. Validation before anything is spent: name 1..32 chars, symbol 1..10 ASCII alphanumerics, description <= 500, links <= 200 each, icon `data:image/`, `https://` or `ipfs://` and <= `max_icon_bytes` (16 KB), quote asset approved and enabled, `fee_mode` in creator/burn/holders (holders on the NEAR pair only), `creator_share_bps` in 5000..9000, `fee_to` not the factory, tax each side <= 400 bps with creator+burn+holders == 10000 and the tax code hash set. The start point is clamped to the quote's own bounds and floored to the 1 % tier's 200-point grid. A dev buy is NEAR pair only; it is capped at the NEAR that buys `max_dev_buy_bps` (4 %) of supply at the start price: at or above cap+1 % it becomes an exact-output buy of exactly 4 % (the exchange refunds the rest), in the 1 % band below the cap it is shaved to cap-1 %, otherwise it runs as requested. The required output (exact amount, or 97 % of the curve's output) is stored per launch (`dm:`) and is the swap's `min_output_amount`.

**Creator deposit** (`quote_launch`, all paid by the creator): `launch_fee` (config, 0 today, booked to `protocol_fees`) + token storage ((3,000 + icon + name/symbol/description [+ 600 for tax] bytes × 1e19 × 1.25) + one 0.00125 N registration for the creator) + 0.1 N attached to `create_pool` + DCL storage (0.5 N for the locker's first registration on an exchange, 0.02 N per launch after) + the capped dev buy. Anything attached above the total is refunded to the creator in the same call, exactly once. The factory pays for its own launch record (including the icon) out of its balance.

**Record**: `id = next_id` (dense, never removed), token account from a per-slug counter (`sc:`; `lock` is reserved, `lock2`/`lock3` seeded), pool id `token_x|token_y|10000` with the pair ordered lexicographically (`token_is_x`), a single-sided range from start+200 up to min(start+range_points, 500,000) (mirrored below the start for a token on the Y side), fee mode and creator share always stored (`fm:`), tax settings with the 20 % platform cut frozen (`tx:`; on a pair-token launch a creator tax share needs fee mode creator), `fee_to` (`fr:`). Steps: `CreateToken → (CreatePool) → AddLiquidity → (DevBuy) → Done`, or `Failed`; `inflight` guards every step.

**CreateToken**: one batch on the token account (create, transfer the storage, `use_global_contract(hash)`, `new(owner = locker, supply, metadata, rules, tax)`, register the creator) runs in parallel with one batch on DCL (locker storage top-up + `create_pool` at the start point). `on_created`: token batch failed → `Failed` (fatal; the dev buy stays held for `refund_failed`); pool created → `AddLiquidity` in the same transaction; pool refused → step `CreatePool` and `on_pool_checked` reads the existing pool: adopted only if its `current_point` equals this launch's start point, otherwise `Failed` (someone pre-created it at another price, or nothing exists); a failed read parks at `CreatePool` for `resume`.

**AddLiquidity**: `locker.add(token, pool_id, range, whole supply on one side)`. lock3 sends the supply as one `ft_transfer_call` carrying DCL's `HotZap` (a zero X→Y swap plus one add_liquidity), then reads the position id back from `list_liquidities` starting at its confirmed-position index. It returns the `lpt_id`, or `""` for "not yet", which parks the launch at `AddLiquidity`; a later `add` searches, then checks the locker's DCL balance, and only re-sends a HotZap after a proven full refund. Gas: when a dev buy follows and the locker has a measured allowance (`set_locker_add_gas`, lock3 = 111 TGas) the locker gets exactly that and the callback 129 TGas; otherwise the locker gets everything spare (never under 180) and the callback 32.

**First buy from the locker (`add_buy`, lock3 with `set_locker_add_buy(locker, true)`)**: when the launch has a funded dev buy not yet made and no `add` or `add_buy` was ever sent for it (`na:` is written when the launch reaches AddLiquidity and removed when either is sent, so the locker has never seen the pool) and 230 + 14 + 5 TGas are left, the AddLiquidity step calls `locker.add_buy(args, {amount, msg})` with the dev buy attached and `dev_buy_held` zeroed first, `msg` being the same `Swap` (floor) or `SwapByOutput` (exact) with `swap_out_recipient = creator` the factory's own dev buy would send. The locker sends the HotZap, and one block later (a hop on itself) wraps the NEAR and sends the swap with that message untouched; a bot that reacts to the position needs a block to see it and one to reach the exchange, so it lands after the creator's buy; a bot that sends a buy every block without looking can still reach the exchange in the swap's block ahead of it, and then the floor refuses the creator's buy and its NEAR comes back (the launch parks at DevBuy, funded). `add_buy` refuses any pool the locker has seen (a retry goes through `add`), holds the NEAR until its hop wraps it, and returns `{lpt_id, used, refunded}` after unwrapping and sending back what the exchange refused; leftovers whose return failed stay in `get_buy_held(token)`. `on_liquidity_added_buy` (14 TGas, no launch hook) books: `used > 0` → the buy is done (`bd:` marks it so no dev buy follows even if the position comes later; an exact buy's output is recorded), `refunded` is the excess, paid to the creator; `used == 0` → `dev_buy_held = refunded`, parked at DevBuy "swap refunded" (then `resume` swaps from the factory, or `cancel_dev_buy`); attached − used − refunded > 0 is recorded in `bh:` as held by the locker; `Err` (add_buy refused, or its last callback died) → `bh:` = the whole buy, `dev_buy_unknown`, parked at AddLiquidity. While `bh:` is set, `cancel_dev_buy`, `refund_dev_buy` and `cancel_stuck_launch` refuse; the owner's `locker_refund_buy(id)` (one in flight per launch) calls lock3's `refund_buy(token)`, which debits what it sends before sending and re-holds a bounce, and what comes back funds the dev buy again while the launch waits, or goes to the creator once it is live or failed; `settle_first_buy(id)` (anyone, see section 2's valves) books an add_buy result that never arrived from lock_5's own record. Once `bd:` is set, whatever `locker_refund_buy` brings back goes to the creator. lock_5 takes the buy off `get_buy_held` when its hop sends it (a reverted batch puts it back), and holds a failed buy's NEAR again only once its return to the factory bounced, so `refund_buy` can never pay NEAR that went into a swap or is already on its way back. In the launch transaction, a launch that wants this buy but has less than that gas (a wallet that caps the transaction, e.g. 270 TGas for EVM wallets, leaves on_created ~220) parks at AddLiquidity before anything reaches the locker, with the reason in `launch_step_parked`; a resume chain's callback parks the same way (3 TGas margin) or when its signer may not trigger a dev buy. `resume` of such a launch then sends `add_buy` itself: the predecessor or signer must be the creator, the owner or a relayer, and 252 TGas must be left, otherwise it panics with nothing changed. Without the flag the launch falls through to `add` and the in-line dev buy below.

**Dev buy in-line (300 TGas)**: `on_liquidity_added` runs the DevBuy step if the gas is there, the signer is the creator, owner or a relayer, the factory is not paused and `dev_buy_held == dev_buy_near > 0`; otherwise it parks with the reason in `launch_step_parked`. `internal_dev_buy` sets `dev_buy_held = 0` before anything is sent, then one batch on wrap.near: registration if needed, `near_deposit(dev_buy_near)`, `ft_transfer_call` to DCL with `Swap` (floor) or `SwapByOutput` (exact), `swap_out_recipient = creator`. The swap gets 80 TGas plus all spare gas; `on_dev_bought` a fixed 36 (55 with 160 + spare from `resume`). Outcomes: used > 0 → `Done`, the unused wNEAR of an exact buy is unwrapped and only then refunded to the creator; used == 0 (refused) → the wNEAR is unwrapped and only a confirmed unwrap re-credits `dev_buy_held`, parking at DevBuy; the wrap batch reverted → the NEAR is back, `dev_buy_held` is re-credited at once.

**Done**: `internal_done` writes the record, emits `launch` and fires the hook (`on_launch`, fixed 10 TGas, no deposit, no callback, only if 20 TGas remain; skipped after an exact dev buy's unwrap).

**Failure and refund paths**
- `refund_failed(id)`: step `Failed` and `dev_buy_held > 0` → held and `dev_buy_near` zeroed, NEAR sent to the creator; a bounce re-credits `dev_buy_held`.
- `cancel_dev_buy(id)` (creator) and `refund_dev_buy(id)` (owner or relayer, for a sniped buy): launch parked at DevBuy and not in flight → goes `Done` without the buy, held NEAR to the creator (never the caller); a bounce is booked to the creator's claimable `creator_fees`.
- `cancel_stuck_launch(id)` (creator, 24 h after `created_at_ms`, parked at CreatePool or AddLiquidity, not in flight): at CreatePool fails at once and refunds; at AddLiquidity asks the locker `get_position(pool_id)` and fails only on a proven "no position" (None, or `lpt_id` null with `refunded` true); anything else keeps the launch (`stuck_launch_kept`). lock and lock2 cannot answer, so their launches are never cancelled this way. A failed launch's token and supply stay in the locker or its exchange balance.
- Owner valves: `set_step` (never a dead step, never off `Done`; Done needs a position, no dev buy money on the launch and nothing held by the locker; DevBuy needs a position; CreateToken/CreatePool/Failed need none), `set_range` (before placement), `reset_inflight`; none of them clears the in-flight flag of a dev buy, at its own step or riding with `add_buy` at AddLiquidity.
- `settle_first_buy(id)` (anyone) books a first buy sent with `add_buy` whose result was lost (callback dead while the buy is in flight) or came back as an error (`bu:`), from the locker's own record (`get_first_buy`) only. No record after an error: the locker never took the NEAR, it came back with the failure, and the dev buy is funded again. A settled record: booked as `on_liquidity_added_buy` books a result (`used`; `returned`, the NEAR the factory saw back; the rest held by the locker). Anything else, or an unreadable record: nothing moves. Its callback re-checks the launch, so two settles, or a settle and the lost callback, never book twice; a late `on_liquidity_added_buy` is acted on only while the first buy is still in flight with the locker (`add_buy_result_ignored`). `locker_refund_buy` waits for the settle. `release_dev_buy(id)` (owner) is the exit for the factory's own dev buy at its DevBuy step whose fixed-gas callback failed: the launch ends at Done and nothing moves (it never goes back in flight); a late `on_dev_bought` / `on_dev_buy_unwrapped` then pays the creator (a fill is booked, a refusal or a reverted wrap is refunded). A dead retried unwrap's `uw:` is cleared so it no longer holds up `sweep_wnear`. A funded buy is never released (`refund_dev_buy` pays it).

## 3. Fees

**Claim**: `claim_fees(id)` (anyone, not paused, `Done`, not in flight) counts an inflow on the token (`sf:`), attaches 180 TGas to `locker.claim(lpt_id, token, token_is_x, quote)` and a callback sized 15 + 6 TGas per payout leg. lock3 calls `remove_liquidity(amount 0, skip_refund_transfer)`, books the released `[x, y]` to that token's `Carry` and to `dcl_reserved`, withdraws exactly this launch's share with `withdraw_asset` (arrival read from DCL's callback result), then forwards what arrived: the token side and an FT quote by `ft_transfer`, a wNEAR quote by `near_withdraw` followed by a native transfer confirmed in `on_delivered`. On the first claim of a pair-token quote, lock_5 reads the quote's `get_tax` (20 TGas view, in place of that claim's quote withdrawal), caches its buy bps per exchange and quote, and from then on books and forwards each quote withdrawal net of that tax (an asset without `get_tax` caches 0). While a claim is open (`get_claims_open`; a count older than 10 minutes since the last claim opened is treated as left by a dead callback), lock_5's owner withdrawal refuses, so fees a claim released but has not booked can never be taken; `on_claimed` never panics. `add`, `add_buy` and `claim` refuse when lock_5 has under 0.1 N free. The value returned to the factory is what the factory received, never what the exchange released; anything not confirmed stays in the launch's own carry for its next claim, and one launch's carry can never be withdrawn or forwarded by another's claim.

**Split** (`on_fees_claimed`): creator share = fee × the launch's stored `creator_share_bps` (config default only for records without one; `backfill_fee_opts` pins those), protocol share = the rest, on both legs. Then:
- Fee mode burn/holders: the quote-side creator share goes to the launch's mode bucket (`mb:`) for the keeper; the token-side creator share is burnable by anyone (`burn_token_fees`).
- House buyback (`set_house_buyback(id)`, owner, one way): a live, not-in-flight launch whose payee is a house creator and whose mode is creator is stored as burn mode with a 10,000 bps share, so from then on every pool fee it earns (both legs, the protocol share included) goes to the mode bucket and `creator_token_fees`, i.e. to `buyback` and `burn_token_fees`; nothing reaches its recipients or the protocol. Refused while the creator has booked token or quote fees on that launch (pay them first). Fees booked before the switch keep their route. Used for $NEARLY (#3) since 2026-10-01.
- Creator mode: the creator share is booked to the payee (`fee_to` if set, else the creator). NEAR pair: `creator_fees[payee]`, or the per-launch router bucket (`rn:`) when the payee is an approved router. FT pair: `creator_quote_fees` on the launch. Token side: `creator_token_fees`.
- A payee that is a house creator (`set_house_creator`, `set_house_creators`) has no third-party creator: its creator share joins the protocol share.
- Protocol share: NEAR into the launch's own bucket (`lb:`) if the launch has recipients, else `protocol_fees`; FT quote into `protocol_quote_fees`; token into `protocol_token_fees`.
- Then, in the same transaction and only if the bucket clears `min_push` (0.05 N) and the gas is there: `protocol_fees` splits to the protocol recipients, the launch bucket to its recipients, router NEAR to the router. `split_legs` gives the last leg the remainder so legs re-sum exactly.

**Push vs claim**: every creator balance can be pulled by the creator (claim, works while paused) or pushed by anyone to the same payee (push, blocked by pause). Token and quote protocol shares are split by anyone (`split_protocol_*`) to the launch's or the global recipients, or withdrawn by the owner. `push_router_fees` and `push_protocol_fees` (owner, ignores the floor) drain the remaining buckets.

**One payout path**: every leg goes through `internal_pay` and returns through `on_paid`. NEAR is a plain transfer; a router is then told `on_launch_fees({launch_id, kind, amount})` with no deposit and no callback, only after the transfer landed. Tokens go to everyone, routers included, by `storage_deposit` + `ft_transfer`, and a router is then told `on_launch_fees` the same way; only a failed transfer (the promise failed) is re-credited, never a value read from the receiver. A failed NEAR transfer re-credits the full amount to the bucket it was debited from; the older per-leg callbacks remain as shims for a leg in flight across the upgrade.

**Buyback and holders**: `buyback(id, min_out)` (keeper, `min_out > 0`) spends the mode bucket (NEAR pair) or `creator_quote_fees` (FT pair) on the launch's own pool, measures the factory's token balance before and after under a per-token swap lock (`sl:`, 10 min expiry, released only by the chain that took it), burns min(difference, 104 % of `min_out`) (nothing when its lock expired or was taken over: `buyback_lock_lost`), counts a pair-token quote as in flight while it is out, and re-credits unused input only after a confirmed unwrap. `pay_holders` / `pay_tax_holders` (keeper) pay a list of at most 25 (NEAR) or 10 (FT) unique accounts whose total is at most the bucket; the pro-rata computation is off-chain. A holder leg that does not arrive is not returned to the pot: it is owed to that holder (`ow:` asset|account, total per asset `ot:`, asset `near` or the pair token; event `holder_owed`), and `push_owed(asset, accounts)` (anyone, not paused, at most 25 for NEAR or 10 for a token, unique) sends each listed account its whole owed balance, skipping accounts owed nothing; a send that fails again stays owed (`owed_push_failed`), one that lands emits `owed_paid`. A pair token that is a launch token is refused while its swap lock is held. Views `get_owed`, `get_owed_many` (at most 200), `get_owed_total`. Balance-difference chains refuse to start while a lock is held or an inflow (`sf:`) is pending.

## 4. Tax

**Token side** (`token-tax`): the tax is fixed at `new`: `buy_bps`/`sell_bps` (the token refuses more than 400 at `new` and clamps at 400 when it applies the tax, whatever the factory asked for), `pairs = [DCL]`, `admin = factory`, `exempt = [locker, tax seller if set at launch]`; the admin and the token itself are always exempt. A transfer out of a pair to a non-exempt receiver is a buy, into a pair from a non-exempt sender a sell; wallet-to-wallet is never taxed. The tax is credited to the token's own account (the vault). On `ft_transfer_call` the tax is added to the in-flight counter (`p`); `ft_resolve_transfer` refunds it to a registered sender in proportion to what came back (all of it for a failed swap, the matching share for a partly used transfer such as an exact-output swap), releases it either way, and reports the gross the sender lost. `tax_take` (admin only) moves vault − in-flight to the factory and returns the amount; `get_tax` shows the same figure as `pending`.

**Token hardening (both tokens)**: arguments are parsed strictly (a looked-up key may appear once, no escaped keys, nothing after the object), account ids follow NEAR's rules, string bodies copied into logs and `ft_on_transfer` args must be valid JSON, memos are capped, the tax is computed as amount × bps / 10,000 without overflow, a transfer to the token account itself is refused, `new` parses its arguments once and refuses rules a transfer would refuse. `ft_resolve_transfer` never panics: it reads the receiver's unused amount the way near-sdk does (a failed, malformed or out-of-range answer refunds everything, an answer over 64 bytes is never copied), returns the tax on what came back pro rata (all of it on a full refund), and releases the in-flight tax either way. `storage_withdraw` succeeds only with no amount or "0" (a registration holds exactly the minimum).

**Factory side**: `collect_tax(id)` (anyone, not paused, `Done`) calls `tax_take` and adds the answer to the launch's pending tax (`tt:`). `process_tax(id, max_amount)` (keeper, not paused, no swap lock; `min_out` and `to_seller` are accepted and ignored) takes pending (or `max_amount`): platform = pending × 20 %, burn = the rest × `burn_bps` (all of it when creator+holders == 0), sell = pending − burn. The burn goes to `token.burn` (a failure returns it to pending); the sell slice is transferred to the tax seller and counted in `selling` (`ts:`; a bounce returns it to pending). The seller settles with `tax_proceeds(id, sold)` plus attached NEAR (NEAR pair) or `ft_transfer_call` of the pair token with msg `{tax_proceeds, sold}` (FT pair, and the predecessor must be that launch's quote). `sold` must be > 0 and at most `selling`; proceeds are split in the same proportions as the tokens (platform : creator : holders = 20 : 80×creator_bps : 80×holders_bps): platform to `protocol_fees` (drained at once) or `protocol_quote_fees`, holders to `th:` for `pay_tax_holders`, creator to the same creator bucket a pool fee uses (`creator_fees`, router bucket, or `creator_quote_fees`). Counters `tb:`, `tc:`, `tp:`, `tq:` record burned and paid totals (`get_tax`). The contract bounds `sold`; the proceeds amount is whatever the seller sends.

## 5. Admin

All owner methods are owner-only, act as soon as they are called and take no attached deposit; the only payable methods are `launch`, `tax_proceeds` and `fund_locker_reserve`. The owner is a Sputnik DAO (README, Admin), so each owner call runs only through a proposal that passes with 3 of 4 council votes. Registrations an owner method needs come out of the factory's balance.

- **Settings**: `set_config` (validated: 1 % tier only, launch fee <= 10 N, exchange storage per launch <= 0.5 N, creator share 5000..9000, icon cap <= 32 KB, dev buy cap <= 20 %, max-wallet window <= 24 h), `set_quote` / `set_quote_enabled` / `register_quote` (at most 64 pair assets; `native` is wrap.near alone; range on the 200 grid and within DCL's liquidity ceiling), `set_protocol_recipients` (<= 4, bps sum 10000, no duplicates; empty = manual withdrawal), `set_house_creator`, `set_house_creators`, `set_launch_recipients` (per launch, 1..4; `None` moves the leftover to `protocol_fees`), `set_min_push`, `set_min_claim`, `set_thresholds`, `set_tax_seller`, `set_relayers`, `set_fee_router`, `set_launch_hook`, `set_locker_from` (future launches only, cutoffs increase, a direct sub-account of the factory with `_` in its name), `set_locker_add_gas` (60..180 TGas, a listed locker only), `set_locker_add_buy` (a listed locker with lock3's `add_buy`), `set_dcl(dcl, migrate)` (new launches only, or a migration that rewrites the schedule and sends `set_dcl` to the lockers on that exchange), `seed_slug_counts` (never lowers), `backfill_fee_opts`, `set_paused`.
- **Code**: `set_token_code_hash`, `set_tax_token_code_hash` (apply to tokens created afterwards), `upgrade(code_hash, migrate)`: `use_global_contract(hash)` on the factory account and, with `migrate`, a call to the new code's `migrate` in the same receipt, so the receipt (code switch included) reverts unless the new code exposes a `migrate` this account can call. The current `migrate` is a private no-op; a version needing a state migration replaces it. Additions since the baseline live under raw keys (3-byte lowercase prefixes listed in the source) so the borsh layout of `Factory`, `Config` and `Launch` never changed.
- **Repairs**: `set_step`, `set_range`, `reset_inflight`, `locker_refund_buy` (a first buy's leftovers, bounded by `bh:`), `settle_first_buy`, `locker_withdraw_dcl_asset(locker, token, amount, max_book)` (brings a locker's loose DCL balance of a launch token or wNEAR here through `withdraw_dcl_asset`, which never dips below the fees launch carries own; the factory books the balance difference under the token's lock, at most `max_book`), `sweep_wnear` (refused while a refused dev buy's wNEAR waits for its unwrap, `uwt` > 0; credited to `protocol_fees` only after the unwrap succeeds), `release_dev_buy`, `push_protocol_fees`.
- **Money out**: `withdraw_protocol_fees(to, amount)`, `withdraw_protocol_token_fees`, `withdraw_protocol_quote_fees`: each moves only the protocol's own booked share and re-credits on a bounce. Nothing lets the owner move a creator, router or launch bucket, or a locker position. Holder buckets are paid by the keeper calls `pay_holders` and `pay_tax_holders` to the list they are given, and the owner passes the keeper check too.
- **Keys**: `add_fc_key(public_key, methods, allowance)` adds a function-call key for calls to this account only, never a full-access key; it refuses an empty method list (the runtime reads it as "any method"), any `on_*` name and `migrate`, any character outside `[A-Za-z0-9_]` (the list reaches the runtime comma-joined), duplicates and a zero allowance. `delete_fc_key` deletes any key of the account, the full-access key included, except the one signing the call. Live layout: no full-access key, and two keeper function-call keys that hold no owner method (README, Admin); `add_fc_key` and `delete_fc_key` run only through the DAO.
- **Ownership**: `propose_owner` (and `transfer_ownership`, same thing) then `accept_owner` by the proposed account; proposing the current owner cancels. Since 2026-10-06 the owner is the DAO `nearlytrade.sputnik-dao.near`, so no key on the factory passes `assert_owner`; keeper methods still accept the factory itself, which is how the keeper keys run them.
- **Pause** (`set_paused`): blocks `launch`, `resume` (except the unwrap of a refused dev buy), the in-line dev buy and the locker-side first buy (a launch reaching it parks), `claim_fees`, `collect_tax`, `process_tax`, `buyback`, `pay_holders`, `pay_tax_holders`, `push_owed`, `tax_proceeds` and the seller's `ft_on_transfer` (refunded), every `push_*`, `split_protocol_*`, `burn_token_fees`. Not blocked: creator claims, `refund_failed`, `cancel_dev_buy`, `refund_dev_buy`, `cancel_stuck_launch`, owner methods, and every callback of a call already in flight.

## 6. Invariants that must always hold

1. Only the factory account can call a locker's `add`, `add_buy`, `refund_buy`, `claim`, `withdraw_dcl_asset`, `set_dcl` or `register`.
2. The locker source contains exactly one `remove_liquidity` call, sent with `amount: "0"`, and no call that transfers, burns or re-ranges a position, adds a key, deploys code or deletes the account.
3. One `add` call sends the supply to the exchange at most once, and never once a position for that pool is recorded or found on the exchange.
4. `claim` returns to the factory only amounts whose arrival at the factory the promise result confirmed; every unconfirmed leg stays in that launch's own carry.
5. A carry is credited and debited only by claims of its own launch token, and `withdraw_dcl_asset` never takes the locker's DCL balance below `dcl_reserved`.
6. Every token account is created with no access keys and code referenced by a global hash, its supply is minted once, and `ft_total_supply` only ever decreases.
7. A token never taxes a transfer whose sender and receiver are both outside `pairs`, never taxes an exempt party, and never applies more than 400 bps a side.
8. A token's vault balance is at least its in-flight tax, and `tax_take` moves at most vault − in-flight, to the admin only.
9. `next_id` only increases, launch ids are dense, and no launch record is ever removed or re-created.
10. A launch at `Done` keeps its `lpt_id` and its step forever: no method, owner methods included, moves a launch off `Done`.
11. A dev buy swap is sent, by the factory or attached to the locker's `add_buy`, only when `dev_buy_held == dev_buy_near > 0`; `dev_buy_held` is zeroed before it is sent, and it is re-credited only by a callback that confirmed the NEAR is back on the factory (a refused swap's confirmed unwrap, the locker's reported `refunded`, or `refund_buy`'s reported return). NEAR the locker still holds is recorded in `bh:` and reachable at any step through `locker_refund_buy`.
12. Across all paths (in-line, resume, refund, cancel) a launch's dev buy NEAR is swapped or refunded at most once in total.
13. Every dev buy swap carries the output floor fixed at launch (the exact 4 % amount, or 97 % of the start-price curve output), never 0.
14. From `resume`, only the creator, the owner or a listed relayer can cause a dev buy swap to be sent, or open the pool of a launch whose first buy is still to go out with its position (`na:` set, funded, add_buy locker); in the launch transaction itself the creator caused it by paying for it, whoever signed.
15. Every dev buy refund is paid to the launch's creator, never to the caller, and a bounced refund is re-booked (to `dev_buy_held` on a failed launch, to `creator_fees` on a live one).
16. `reset_inflight`, `set_step` and `set_range` never clear the in-flight flag of a dev buy: a launch at DevBuy, or at AddLiquidity with `dev_buy_near > 0` and `dev_buy_held == 0` (its buy riding with `add_buy`).
17. A refused `create_pool` adopts an existing pool only when its current point equals the launch's start point; otherwise the launch fails and its dev buy becomes refundable.
18. `cancel_stuck_launch` never marks a launch `Failed` while the locker may hold a position for it.
19. Every NEAR, token or quote leg that leaves the factory is debited from a bucket before the transfer and, on failure, exactly the returned amount is re-credited to that same bucket, except a holder's payout leg, whose returned amount is owed to that holder (and `push_owed` re-books a failed re-send the same way).
20. The sum of `protocol_fees`, all `creator_fees`, all launch, mode, router, tax-holder buckets, the NEAR owed to holders (`ot:near`) and all `dev_buy_held` never exceeds the factory account's liquid NEAR balance.
21. A NEAR-denominated bucket is credited only from NEAR that has arrived: pool fees after the locker's confirmed transfer, attached deposits in the same call, wNEAR only after a confirmed unwrap.
22. An FT-quote fee is never booked to a NEAR bucket, and holders mode is never granted on an FT pair.
23. A launch's fee mode, creator share, tax split, `fee_to` and platform cut are fixed at launch and never change afterwards, with one exception: `set_house_buyback` may move a house-paid creator-mode launch to burn mode at 10,000 bps, once, and nothing moves it back.
24. `pay_holders` and `pay_tax_holders` pay a list of unique accounts whose total is at most the launch's bucket; `withdraw_protocol_fees` never exceeds `protocol_fees`; `split_legs` re-sums to its input exactly; `push_owed` pays an account at most what it is owed in that asset, and only to that account.
25. `buyback` requires `min_out > 0`, burns at most min(measured difference, 104 % × `min_out`), and holds the launch token's swap lock from the balance read to the burn callback.
26. No balance-difference chain starts on a token while a swap lock is held or an inflow is pending on it, and the seller's pair-token settlement is refused while that token's swap lock is held.
27. `process_tax` hands the seller at most the pending tax it debited, and a settlement is accepted only from the tax seller, for `sold` at most what that launch still has with the seller, with the proceeds split summing exactly to the amount received.
28. No `on_*` callback and no `migrate` is callable through a key added by `add_fc_key`, and `add_fc_key` never adds a full-access key.
29. Every owner method executes with zero attached deposit, and ownership changes only when the proposed account calls `accept_owner`.
30. `upgrade(hash, migrate: true)` leaves the old code in place unless the new code exposes a `migrate` the factory account can call.
31. Pause never blocks a creator claim, a dev buy refund, a stuck-launch exit, an owner method or a callback already in flight.
32. `fee_to`, a fee router, the launch hook and the exchange can never be the factory account itself, and every owner-set list is bounded (recipients 4, relayers 16, routers 32, quotes 64, payouts 25/10, view ids 200).

## 7. Known limitations and accepted risks

- **Owner is a 3 of 4 DAO with no timelock.** The owner is the Sputnik DAO `nearlytrade.sputnik-dao.near` (README, Admin). A passed proposal acts at once: it can upgrade the factory (`upgrade_code`, `upgrade`), redirect future fees, drain `protocol_fees` and the factory's own float, list fake launches, give future launches other token or locker code, and add taxed pair accounts to tax tokens that support it (`token_add_pair`, add only, at the token's fixed launch rate). Through an upgrade it could also take the creator and holder balances the factory holds. It cannot mint, change a token's code or tax rate, or move a locker position. Pausing also needs 3 approvals, and a vote cannot be changed once cast.
- **Rhea dependency.** Positions, pools and fee accounting live on `dclv2.ref-labs.near`, an upgradable third-party contract; the gas floors and result formats (`withdraw_asset` returns `true`, `HotZap` semantics, `list_liquidities` order) were measured against v2.3.13/2.3.14 and would need re-measuring after a Rhea upgrade. `set_dcl(migrate)` is fire-and-forget to the lockers.
- **Tax seller trust.** The seller reports the proceeds amount; the contract checks only that `sold` is bounded. Its key lives on a server. Tokens created before the seller was set (or before it was made exempt) tax the seller's own sale back into the vault, which is collected again.
- **Tax on the dev buy.** On a tax launch the creator's dev buy pays the buy tax, so the creator receives up to 4 % less than the floor checked at the exchange.
- **Exact dev buys skip the hook** when their unwrap uses the gas margin (`launch_hook_skipped`); the hook is best effort in general (fixed gas, no retry).
- **Pre-created pool grief.** Anyone can create the pool for a predictable token name at another price; the launch fails, the dev buy is refundable, but the token storage, pool and DCL deposits are spent and the supply stays in the locker.
- **Failed launches leave a token** with its supply in the locker or on the exchange; `locker_withdraw_dcl_asset` can only bring loose DCL balance to the factory's protocol buckets.
- **Locks expire after 10 minutes.** A chain that outlives the expiry could overlap another balance-difference chain on the same token.
- **Off-chain keepers** compute holder pro-rata lists, buyback floors and claim scheduling; the contract bounds totals but not the fairness of a list.
- **`add_buy` timing.** The locker's first buy lands one block after the HotZap; a bot that pre-creates the pool or spams swaps every block against a pool it cannot yet see can at best land in the HotZap block, where there is no liquidity. The buy still pays the pool fee and, on a tax launch, the buy tax.
- **Router trust.** Routers are paid by plain transfer and told by a notice with no callback, so a router that ignores the notice is not detected on chain; no router return value is ever read as a refund.
- **Token JSON parser.** The tokens use a minimal hand-written parser: duplicate keys take the first value, trailing garbage is accepted, `memo`/`msg` are copied into events without re-escaping, and `ft_resolve_transfer` panics on an empty or over-long receiver result (see the fuzz harness in `contracts/token-tax/sim`). `auto_register` spends the token's own balance to register DCL payout recipients until a margin is reached.
- **Icon storage** is paid by the factory for its own launch record; the creator pays only the token's copy.
- **FT-quote launches** have no dev buy, no holders mode, settle as FT balances, and need `register_quote` before their first claim or the fees strand in DCL's inner account.
- **Unit tests** run on near-sdk's mock with a heavier fee table than mainnet; the gas figures come from a local neard sandbox with the real exchange wasm.

## 8. Out of scope, and earlier generations still live

- `lock3`, `lock4` and `lock_4` (launches 1810..1930, 1931..1934, 1935 up to the lock_5 cutoff): earlier revisions of `contracts/locker3`, zero keys; lock3 and lock4 lack `add_buy`, and none of the three has the open-claim guard, the funded guard or the quote-tax probe (pair-token quotes with a transfer tax are kept whole there by an off-chain top-up).
- `lock.nearlytrade.near` (launches 0..157) and `lock2.nearlytrade.near` (158 up to the lock3 cutoff): earlier locker code (`contracts/locker` is lock2's source, kept for reference), zero keys, still serving claims for their launches through the same factory calls; they lack `get_position`, `set_dcl`, `withdraw_dcl_asset` and `add_buy`, so `cancel_stuck_launch`, a DCL migration and the locker-side first buy do not reach them.
- Earlier token codes: every existing token stays on the global code it was created with; tax tokens created before the in-flight fix can have a failed swap's refunded tax collected by `tax_take`.
- Dead `Step` variants (`Deposit`, `ReadDevTokens`, `DeliverDevTokens`) and the per-leg `on_*` shims exist only for records and receipts from the previous code and are refused or inert.
- Rhea DCL, `wrap.near`, third-party routers and the launch hook, the tax seller's trading, relayers, cranks, the indexer and the website.
- Key ceremony, deployment and operations procedures.
